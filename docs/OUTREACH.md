# Evidence-based engagement path

## The qualifying chain

```text
publicly documented problem category
  → appropriate, reachable channel
  → recipient confirms their actual problem and interest
  → mutually authorized redacted/synthetic evaluation
  → preregistered comparison and held-out results
  → voluntary scoped proposal
  → agreed contract/payment terms
```

A category fit is not buyer demand. A public address is not a warm introduction. A VC invests in companies; it is not automatically a customer for an individual audit. This repository creates no duty to purchase or implement. No claim is made about a “Black October” incident or a specific organization’s unresolved vulnerability without primary evidence.

## First contact, prepared but not sent

Use a narrowly truthful founder-program enquiry, not a claim that the recipient has been breached or must buy. The private sending workflow must show the owner the complete recipient, subject, body and final repository link before sending. Do not place private correspondence or personal contacts in this public repository.

Proposed subject: `PRIME prototype: eligibility for a no-fee evaluation route?`

> I’m an independent AI-evaluation researcher developing PRIME, a local source-paired text audit tool. I’ve published a reproducible security demonstrator with 18 synthetic scenarios, 26 regression tests, explicit failure cases and a separate mock authorization gate: https://github.com/socio-sapien/prime-security-demonstrator
>
> It does not claim to neutralize malware. The testable question is whether its inspectable evidence helps a practitioner identify source-to-summary changes and review suspicious tool requests more accurately or quickly.
>
> Does your program have a no-fee route for an independent researcher at this prototype stage to a practitioner-led evaluation using synthetic or redacted examples? If so, I’d appreciate the appropriate next step.
>
> Nathaniel Michael Jaffe-Norris

No name, organization or apparent match establishes enthusiasm or buying interest. This message asks about access to evaluation; it does not seek or accept paid membership, a contract or an investment.

## Audited sequence, not an autonomous mass campaign

1. Recompute tests and evidence; stop on a regression or unsupported claim. `outreach_plan.py` checks local evidence and records a dry-run preflight.
2. Validate recipient/channel and eligibility from the official page. Keep delivery authority separate from the public demo.
3. Present the exact first contact for the owner’s required send review. Until authenticated approval, status is `READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW`, not sent.
4. After a confirmed send, record it privately. Do not infer delivery/opening or start a blind follow-up chain.
5. On an actual reply, classify interest/decline/paid-program offer and prepare the corresponding answer. A decline stops contact. A paid program requires a separate explicit purchase decision.
6. Only after a practitioner confirms a real problem and consents, prepare an evaluation with the bounded protocol in `EVALUATION.md`.
7. Only after results and scope are accepted, prepare voluntary fees, contract and payment terms. None of these stages is currently established.

The local preflight simulates three routes: unapproved send (blocked), prepared owner-review package (ready for review), and a sales close without a buyer (blocked). It cannot send email, apply to programs, crawl social profiles, contact executives or initiate payments. Later steps are gated on actual external evidence, not a fictional simulation of enthusiastic responses.
